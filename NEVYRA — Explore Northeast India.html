Getting around the Northeast

Build routes around the region's real geography: air gateways for longer distances, rail for connected corridors, and road travel for the final leg into valleys, villages and hill destinations.

✈️ Air

Use major gateways such as Guwahati and other regional airports to shorten long inter-state journeys. Check seasonal schedules.

🚆 Rail

Rail is useful on established corridors, especially around Assam and gateway cities. Continue onward by road where hill access is required.

🚐 Road

Shared taxis, state/private buses and hired vehicles are important for hill destinations and the final-mile journey.

🧭 Permits

Some routes or states can require permits or special entry formalities (ILP/PAP). Check the relevant official tourism portal before departure.

Responsible travel

Leave lighter.
Connect deeper.

Responsible tourism is not an extra feature. It is part of the product experience.

🌸

Respect culture

Learn local etiquette and ask before photographing people, ceremonies or sacred spaces.

🌲

Protect nature

Follow marked routes, minimise waste and avoid disturbing wildlife and fragile habitats.

🤝

Support local

Choose local guides, stays, food and crafts where possible.

🧭

Travel informed

Check weather, road conditions, permits, local advisories and seasonal access before departure.
